# Business rules

This page summarizes invariants enforced by server services. Constants and service validation are authoritative when a value changes. HTTP routes and request shapes are documented in [API Contracts](ApiContracts.md).

## Accounts and holders

### Opening and eligibility

- Opening balance must meet the selected account type's minimum. New accounts start active.
- A customer may not open another active individual account of the same product. Account type eligibility also checks allowed customer types and any configured `RequiredProductId` prerequisite.
- Every holder must resolve to a persisted customer whose KYC status is `Verified`.
- An individual account has one holder with 100% ownership. A joint account has two distinct holders, exactly one primary holder, positive ownership percentages totaling 100%, and a shared signing rule.
- Product referrer minimums are evaluated against holder type. Each supplied referrer must be a distinct existing customer who already owns an account.
- Account numbers are generated by the server in `TTyyyyMMddHHSSSS` form: product ID, Myanmar local hour, and a per-product/per-hour sequence.

### Status and updates

- Active accounts may become Dormant, Suspended, Frozen, or Closed. Dormant accounts may become Active, Suspended, or Frozen. Suspended and Frozen accounts may become Active. Closed accounts are terminal.
- Status changes retain the prior/new status, reason, actor, and UTC time. The account tracks its latest entry time for supported statuses.
- Joint-holder updates change the complete editable state of the existing holders. They cannot add, remove, or replace customers, and cannot be applied after closure.
- Account, holder, and fixed-deposit mutations use optimistic versions. A stale version rejects the operation; successful writes increment affected versions.

## Fixed deposits and documents

- Fixed-deposit behavior is selected by the product's `FIXED` category. Creation requires an applicable interest rule, renewal instruction, and calculation-source choice.
- The calculation-source choice is immutable after creation. The HTTP update surface is limited to payout account and renewal instruction; lifecycle operations are service workflows.
- A payout account must be active, individually owned by the primary holder, and not itself a fixed-deposit product. If omitted at creation, the server selects an eligible account. Product-opening prerequisites do not constrain payout-account selection.
- Closed and Cancelled deposits are terminal. Current principal cannot be negative. Renewable maturity creates a successor using the applicable rate, term, payout account, and renewal settings. Renewal principal follows the stored calculation-source choice.
- Required account documents are defined by product configuration. Each document type can be supplied once; allowed formats and size limits are enforced by upload validation. Stored files use private generated references and begin pending verification.

## Customers and KYC

- Customer creation and updates share field validation and duplicate checks. Duplicate identifiers such as NRC, passport, or email are rejected according to the service's uniqueness rules.
- Customer document updates can add documents or edit documents already owned by that customer. A document ID from another customer is invalid; new documents need a type.
- KYC review is a distinct action and must be performed by an eligible manager. Approval stamps verification attribution on the customer's documents; rejection changes the KYC decision without claiming document verification.
- Account opening independently rechecks customer resolution and verified KYC, even if a client hid or skipped its own checks.

## Transactions and ledger

### Common posting rules

- Transaction amounts must be positive and fit the configured decimal precision. Request text, dates, paging values, and account combinations are validated by the transaction services.
- Closed, Frozen, and Suspended accounts cannot send or receive funds. Dormant accounts may be used for teller postings and are reactivated with status history; scheduled interest, fee, and dormant-penalty postings do not reactivate them.
- Product capabilities govern deposits, withdrawals, and transfers. Minimum deposit/withdrawal amounts, minimum maintained balance, available funds, and daily/weekly/monthly customer debit limits apply where configured.
- Financial postings are idempotent when an `Idempotency-Key` is supplied as required by the endpoint. A key cannot be reused for a different request.
- Balance changes go through the ledger posting workflow. Customer and general-ledger entries must balance; refunds and linked account transactions are recorded atomically with their originating workflow.

### Transfer lifecycle

- Internal transfers post both sides in one operation. Interbank and NRC transfers can remain pending until completed, paid out, or cancelled under their respective workflows.
- Pending transfers cannot be completed twice. Failure/cancellation refunds the source under the applicable rules. NRC pickup uses a protected pickup code; reissue invalidates the previous code.
- Transfer fees and account-type restrictions are evaluated by the server. A caller cannot make an otherwise forbidden debit by choosing a different client screen.

### Cash custody

- A manager explicitly starts the current Myanmar business date after all earlier dates are closed, entering the starting vault cash in the same operation; the date and its vault session are created atomically. Reads do not create dates, and End of Day close does not start the next date.
- Managers create teller sessions assigned to one active officer. Teller sessions include Myanmar local start and end times; an officer can activate only within that period, and unactivated sessions automatically close after the end time. An officer can have multiple assigned sessions but only one active session at a time.
- Teller cash postings are attached server-side to the actor's active teller session for the active business date. The client does not select the cash session for a transaction. Until activation, reads and self password changes remain available while session-gated writes are rejected.
- Cash proposals reserve available vault cash when approved. Receipt transfers reserved cash to the teller; rejection releases the reservation. Every vault decrease preserves a nonnegative available balance, including funds reserved for other proposals.
- Closing a teller session can route counted cash to the vault through a manager-acknowledged handoff. The vault is credited only on acknowledgement; existing teller-to-teller cash movements and handoffs remain supported.
- Physical counts are immutable observations and do not overwrite expected cash. Adjustments require a signed amount tied to the relevant posted Cash on Hand journal effect and independent approval.
- Cash movement, transaction posting, and their audit data are coordinated transactionally. Handoffs track custody and require eligible recipients and valid state transitions.

## Accounting and business dates

- Every posted financial transaction has balanced journal entries. Reconciliation compares operational account balances with customer-liability ledger balances and retains run/result snapshots and exception history.
- Journal validation checks the exact GL account, direction, amount, and customer account for every line. Transfer fees add matching customer-deposit and fee-income lines; interbank refunds return the original fee through matching fee-income and customer-deposit lines. Customer-deposit journal lines are reconciled to account transactions by account, direction, and amount.
- A reconciliation exception can be resolved only after a rerun confirms the difference is cleared; direct resolution is not an allowed substitute for evidence.
- The business date is persisted and distinct from UTC transaction timestamps. Myanmar local time determines the banking date and relevant calendar boundaries.
- Scheduled reconciliation, maintenance, and interest use the scheduled occurrence's Myanmar business date; they do not require an open business date or cash session. Scheduled cash operations must still follow the active business-date and cash-session rules.
- When multiple business dates are open from legacy data or an interrupted close, End of Day must process the oldest open date first; closing a later open date is rejected until earlier dates are closed.
- End-of-day close is a staged, authorized process with pre-close checks, distinct approval, and final rechecks under lock. Open and scheduled teller sessions and unresolved cash handoffs block close. Blocking conditions must be resolved or handled through the explicit manager override workflow.
- A single-manager force-close request and its automatic close are committed in one database transaction, so the override cannot remain approved if the close fails.
- Monthly accounting close depends on the prior month's final business date being closed. Scheduled-job retry replays an eligible occurrence; it does not close dates or bypass accounting gates.

## Users, roles, and permissions

- Role and permission checks are enforced by the server. Hiding a desktop action is not authorization.
- User creation/update validates role assignment, username/email uniqueness, and status. Passwords are hashed through the shared password utility; reset credentials and forced-change behavior follow the user-management service.
- Soft deletion preserves historical references. The last active manager cannot be removed or demoted when that would leave the system without an active manager.
- Auditing records the authenticated actor and is committed with the operation it describes.

## Scheduled work

- Job occurrences and attempts are persisted. Database leases prevent multiple server instances from executing the same occurrence concurrently.
- Automatic retries and manager-requested retries follow configured limits and eligibility checks. A retry uses the same scheduled occurrence and period; it is not a new business event.
- Financial job handlers must use the same posting and audit rules as interactive operations.

## Change checklist

When changing a rule, update its service/constants first, then the relevant API contract and this page. Update [Message Codes](MessageCodes.md) if callers receive a new or changed code. Keep the OpenAPI contract in sync with public request/response changes.
